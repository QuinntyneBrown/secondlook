Overview

SecondLook speaks plainly to someone about to spend thousands on a used car. The tone is calm, specific and honest: a clear number, a clear next step, no jargon. Use “safety certified” in Ontario-facing copy and “pass a safety inspection” elsewhere.

Voice and tone

MomentToneExample
BookingReassuring, briefPick a time that suits you and the seller.
During the callNeutral, factualMechanic is sharing video.
EstimateDirect, no hedgingAbout $640 to get this car certified.
ErrorCalm, solution firstWe couldn't hold that slot. Choose another time.
RefundPlain, no fine print feelYou're refunded in full.

Casing and grammar

  • Sentence case for headings, buttons, labels and menu items.
  • Buttons start with a verb: Book an inspection, Add another car, Download report.
  • Address the reader as “you”; the product as “we”.
  • No exclamation marks except celebration after payment.

Error message formula

What happened + what to do, in the user's words. Never blame the user, never show raw codes without a plain sentence.

Enter an email like name@example.com.

Specific, fixable, next to the field.
HTML
<div class="field"><label class="field__label" for="c-e">Email</label><input class="input" id="c-e" aria-invalid="true" aria-describedby="c-e-err" value="sam@example"><p class="field__error" id="c-e-err">Enter an email like name@example.com.</p></div>

Empty state formula

Title says what is missing; one sentence says why or when it fills; one primary action moves forward. Example: “No receipts yet. Receipts appear here after your first booking. [Book an inspection]”.
