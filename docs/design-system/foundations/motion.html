Foundation · Behaviour

Motion

Short durations, two curves and a single switch that honours reduced motion.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable
  • Overview
  • Durations
  • Easings
  • Choreography
  • Reduced motion
  • Accessibility
  • Tokens

Overview

Motion explains a change; it never decorates. Durations are short (120–320ms for UI, 500ms for deliberate transitions) and use a decelerating curve on enter and an accelerating one on exit.

Choreography

InteractionDurationEasing
Hover, focus colour change--duration-fast--ease-standard
Switch, tab underline, accordion--duration-base--ease-standard
Dialog, drawer, toast enter--duration-base to --duration-slow--ease-enter
Dialog, drawer, toast exit--duration-fast--ease-exit

Reduced motion

When prefers-reduced-motion: reduce is set, every duration token becomes 0.01ms in tokens.css, so every component calms down without per-component code. Smooth scrolling is turned off as well. The demos above stop moving under that setting.

Accessibility

Nothing flashes more than three times per second (2.3.1). Auto-dismissing toasts last at least 8 seconds, pause on hover and focus, and errors never auto-dismiss (2.2.1). The live-call pulse dot is paired with the text “Live”.
