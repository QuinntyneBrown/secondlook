Dialogs and overlays

When to use a dialog, drawer, popover, menu or a page.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable
  • Overview
  • When
  • Structure
  • States
  • Responsive
  • Accessibility
  • Content

Overview

Overlays interrupt. Use the lightest one that does the job and keep focus handled the same way for all modal ones.

When to use which

NeedUse
Confirm a destructive actionDialog (small)
Short form from a buttonDialog (medium)
Filters or notifications beside the pageDrawer
One-line helpTooltip
Brief help with a linkPopover
Secondary actionsMenu
Anything longA page

Structure

Delete account?

This removes your inspections and reports.

States

  • Stack at most one modal; a toast may sit above it.
  • Busy dialogs disable Cancel and Esc until the action ends.

Responsive behaviour

Dialogs are bottom sheets on phones and centred panels from the medium breakpoint.

Accessibility

Modal: role="dialog" aria-modal="true", labelled, focus trapped, Esc closes, focus returns to the trigger, page inert. Button-triggered editing always opens a dialog or navigates to a screen; there are no inline forms in pages.

Content

Titles are questions or verb phrases; buttons repeat the verb.
