Notifications

Toast, banner and the notification list: priority, grouping and dismissal.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Notifications are ordered by urgency: banners for ongoing state, toasts for moments, the notification page for history.

When to use which

PrioritySurface
Needs a decision nowDialog
Ongoing stateBanner (persistent)
Just happenedToast
Happened earlierNotification list

Structure

Report is ready
View report

States

  • Stacked toasts show newest at the bottom; at most three at once.
  • Errors never auto-dismiss; others last 8 seconds and pause on hover and focus.

Responsive behaviour

Toasts are full width on phones and anchored bottom-left from the medium breakpoint.

Accessibility

Polite status for info and success, assertive alert for errors; dismiss buttons are labelled; nothing disappears while focused.

Content

One line of what happened; one action at most.
