Stepper

Shows progress through a short multi-step flow such as booking.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Use for flows of three to six steps where order matters. It reports progress; it does not navigate.

Anatomy

  1. Step markerNumber, or a check when complete.
  2. LabelShort noun.
  3. Currentaria-current="step".

Variants

Booking progress.

  1. Vehicle
  2. Review
  3. Schedule
  4. Pay
Horizontal
HTML
<ol class="stepper" aria-label="Booking progress" style="width:100%"><li data-complete><span>Vehicle</span></li><li data-complete><span>Review</span></li><li aria-current="step"><span>Schedule</span></li><li><span>Pay</span></li></ol>

Sizes

One size.

States

Pointer and focus states are forced here with data-state so they can be reviewed without a mouse; real pages get them from :hover, :focus-visible and :active. Attribute states (aria-*, disabled) are the real ones.

Responsive behaviour

Navigation collapses to the smallest pattern that keeps every destination reachable: wrapped rows on phones, inline from the medium breakpoint.

Theming

Colours come from semantic roles, so the component flips with the theme and needs no dark-mode rules of its own. Re-theme it by overriding its component tokens on a parent, never by editing the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Ordered list with aria-current="step".

Keyboard

KeyAction
—Not interactive.

Focus

Not focusable; the page heading announces the step.

Labelling

List named “Booking progress”; completion is announced as text.

Contrast

Current label
Complete marker
Upcoming label

Motion

Colour and background transitions use --duration-fast; under prefers-reduced-motion they are effectively instant.

Touch

Hit area is at least --target-min (24px) and 44px for primary mobile actions.

Content

One-word steps: Vehicle, Review, Schedule, Pay.
