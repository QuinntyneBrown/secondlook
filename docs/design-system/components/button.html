Button

Buttons trigger an action on the current page. Five variants, three sizes, every state.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Use a button to do something (book, pay, download, cancel). Use a link to go somewhere. There is one primary button per view; the rest are secondary or ghost. Danger is reserved for destructive confirmations such as cancelling a paid inspection.

Anatomy

  1. Leading icon (optional)20px, inherits the label colour, aria-hidden.
  2. LabelA verb phrase in sentence case; it is the accessible name.
  3. ContainerHeight from --control-height-*, --radius-md, 1px border.

Variants

Variants change emphasis, not meaning. Pick by how much attention the action deserves on this view.

Sizes

Medium is the default. Small lives in tables and toolbars; large is for auth and the booking confirmation.

Heights: --control-height-sm/md/lg.
HTML
<button class="btn btn--primary btn--sm" type="button">Small · 32px</button><button class="btn btn--primary" type="button">Medium · 40px</button><button class="btn btn--primary btn--lg" type="button">Large · 48px</button>

States

Pointer and focus states are forced here with data-state so they can be reviewed without a mouse; real pages get them from :hover, :focus-visible and :active. Attribute states (aria-*, disabled) are the real ones.

Responsive behaviour

Controls keep their height at every width. In a row they wrap onto a new line rather than shrink; on phones primary actions go full width (.btn--block) at the bottom of the form.

Theming

Colours come from semantic roles, so the component flips with the theme and needs no dark-mode rules of its own. Re-theme it by overriding its component tokens on a parent, never by editing the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native <button> (APG Button). Toggle buttons add aria-pressed; menu buttons add aria-haspopup and aria-expanded.

Keyboard

KeyAction
EnterActivates the control.
SpaceActivates the control.
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to or from the control in reading order.

Focus

The shared 2px ring appears on :focus-visible only. Loading keeps focus on the button.

Labelling

The visible label is the name. Icon-only buttons need aria-label. Loading sets aria-busy="true" and keeps the width.

Motion

Colour and background transitions use --duration-fast; under prefers-reduced-motion they are effectively instant.

Touch

Hit area is at least --target-min (24px) and 44px for primary mobile actions.

Content

Start with a verb: Book inspection, Add another car, Download report. Sentence case. Keep to one to three words; say what happens, not “Submit”.
