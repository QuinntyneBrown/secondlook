Menu

A dropdown list of actions opened from a button.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Use a menu for three to eight secondary actions that do not deserve their own button, such as row actions. Do not use it for navigation between pages (use tabs or a nav).

Anatomy

  1. TriggerA button with aria-haspopup="menu" and aria-expanded.
  2. Menurole="menu", raised surface, shadow level 3.
  3. Itemrole="menuitem", 40px tall.
  4. DividerSeparates groups; danger items go last.

Variants

Items can be neutral or danger.

Sizes

Menu width is content-driven (min 12rem).

States

Pointer and focus states are forced here with data-state so they can be reviewed without a mouse; real pages get them from :hover, :focus-visible and :active. Attribute states (aria-*, disabled) are the real ones.

Responsive behaviour

Controls keep their height at every width. In a row they wrap onto a new line rather than shrink; on phones primary actions go full width (.btn--block) at the bottom of the form.

Theming

Colours come from semantic roles, so the component flips with the theme and needs no dark-mode rules of its own. Re-theme it by overriding its component tokens on a parent, never by editing the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

APG Menu Button: trigger opens a role="menu"; focus moves to the first item.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Enter / Space / ↓Opens the menu, focuses the first item.
↑ / ↓Moves between items; wraps.
Home / EndFirst or last item.
Type a letterJumps to the matching item.
EscCloses and returns focus to the trigger.
TabCloses the menu and moves on.

Focus

Focus is inside the menu while open and returns to the trigger on close.

Labelling

The trigger text names the menu; each item has visible text.

Motion

Colour and background transitions use --duration-fast; under prefers-reduced-motion they are effectively instant.

Touch

Hit area is at least --target-min (24px) and 44px for primary mobile actions.

Content

Items are verbs. Put the destructive item last, separated by a divider.
