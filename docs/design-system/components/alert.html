Alert and banner

Overview

Use an alert inside content for something the reader must notice. Use a banner for page-level, persistent notices such as the window countdown.

Anatomy

  1. IconTone.
  2. TitleShort.
  3. BodyExplanation.
  4. ActionsLinks.

States

Pointer and focus states are forced here with data-state so they can be reviewed without a mouse; real pages get them from :hover, :focus-visible and :active. Attribute states (aria-*, disabled) are the real ones.

Responsive behaviour

Feedback is full-width on phones and anchored to a corner from the medium breakpoint, never covering the focused control.

Theming

Colours come from semantic roles, so the component flips with the theme and needs no dark-mode rules of its own. Re-theme it by overriding its component tokens on a parent, never by editing the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Plain content; role="alert" only for new, important content, else role="status".

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to or from the control in reading order.
EnterActivates the control.

Focus

Links inside are focusable; the alert is not.

Labelling

Tone is in the words, not just colour.
