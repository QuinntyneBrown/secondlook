Progress bar

Shows how far a task has progressed.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Use determinate when you know the percentage (upload), indeterminate when you do not.

Anatomy

  1. TrackSubtle.
  2. BarAccent fill.

Variants

Determinate, indeterminate, success.

Bars
HTML
<div style="width:100%;display:grid;gap:var(--space-3)"><div class="progress" role="progressbar" aria-label="Upload" aria-valuenow="60" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100"><div class="progress__bar" style="--value:60%"></div></div><div class="progress progress--indeterminate" role="progressbar" aria-label="Preparing report"><div class="progress__bar"></div></div><div class="progress progress--success" role="progressbar" aria-label="Done" aria-valuenow="100" aria-valuemin="0" aria-valuemax="100"><div class="progress__bar" style="--value:100%"></div></div></div>

Sizes

One thickness (8px).

States

Pointer and focus states are forced here with data-state so they can be reviewed without a mouse; real pages get them from :hover, :focus-visible and :active. Attribute states (aria-*, disabled) are the real ones.

VariantDefault
Determinate

Both themes

Responsive behaviour

Feedback is full-width on phones and anchored to a corner from the medium breakpoint, never covering the focused control.

Theming

Colours come from semantic roles, so the component flips with the theme and needs no dark-mode rules of its own. Re-theme it by overriding its component tokens on a parent, never by editing the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="progressbar" with values, or aria-busy when indeterminate.
