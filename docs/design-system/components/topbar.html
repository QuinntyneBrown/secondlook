Top bar

The app header: brand, primary navigation and account actions.

Overview

One top bar per page. On phones the primary navigation drops to its own scrollable row. The header holds the brand link, the current-page nav and the account menu.

Anatomy

  1. BrandLogo mark plus wordmark; links home.
  2. Primary navnav landmark with aria-current="page".
  3. Account actionsAvatar or sign-in button.

Signed-in and signed-out.

Sizes

Height is fixed by --layout-topbar-height.

States

Pointer and focus states are forced here with data-state so they can be reviewed without a mouse; real pages get them from :hover, :focus-visible and :active. Attribute states (aria-*, disabled) are the real ones.

Responsive behaviour

Below the medium breakpoint the primary nav wraps to its own horizontally scrollable row beneath the brand row; from medium it sits inline.

Theming

Colours come from semantic roles, so the component flips with the theme and needs no dark-mode rules of its own. Re-theme it by overriding its component tokens on a parent, never by editing the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

<header> with a <nav aria-label="Primary">; current page uses aria-current="page".

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to or from the control in reading order.
EnterActivates the control.

Focus

Skip link is the first tab stop; nav links show the ring.

Labelling

Brand link has accessible text; avatar has a role and label.

Contrast

Nav link on surface
Current link
Focus ring on surface

Motion

Colour and background transitions use --duration-fast; under prefers-reduced-motion they are effectively instant.

Touch

Hit area is at least --target-min (24px) and 44px for primary mobile actions.

Content

Nav labels are single nouns: Inspections, Compare, Help.
