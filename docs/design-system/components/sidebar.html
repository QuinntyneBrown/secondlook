Sidebar navigation

Secondary navigation for settings and account areas.

Overview

Use the sidebar when a section has more than four sibling pages (settings). On phones it hides behind a menu button.

Anatomy

  1. Group labelOverline text.
  2. Link.nav-link filling the width.
  3. Current itemaria-current="page"

Variants

Grouped links.

Sizes

Width is a layout token.

States

Pointer and focus states are forced here with data-state so they can be reviewed without a mouse; real pages get them from :hover, :focus-visible and :active. Attribute states (aria-*, disabled) are the real ones.

Responsive behaviour

Navigation collapses to the smallest pattern that keeps every destination reachable: wrapped rows on phones, inline from the medium breakpoint.

Theming

Colours come from semantic roles, so the component flips with the theme and needs no dark-mode rules of its own. Re-theme it by overriding its component tokens on a parent, never by editing the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A nav landmark with a name; current page aria-current.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to or from the control in reading order.
EnterActivates the control.

Focus

Ring around the link.

Labelling

Landmark has an aria-label distinct from the primary nav.

Contrast

Current text
Group label
Focus ring on surface

Motion

Colour and background transitions use --duration-fast; under prefers-reduced-motion they are effectively instant.

Touch

Hit area is at least --target-min (24px) and 44px for primary mobile actions.

Content

Group labels are nouns; links name the page.
