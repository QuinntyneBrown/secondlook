Overview

Use radios for two to five options the reader should compare. The review-type control is a card-style radio group.

Anatomy

  1. LegendNames the question.
  2. Radio20px circle, dot when selected.
  3. LabelClickable text.

Vertical radios and the segmented review-type variant.

States

Pointer and focus states are forced here with data-state so they can be reviewed without a mouse; real pages get them from :hover, :focus-visible and :active. Attribute states (aria-*, disabled) are the real ones.

Responsive behaviour

Inputs fill their container (never fixed width) and stack one per row below the medium breakpoint. Heights stay at 40px or more so touch targets are comfortable.

Theming

Colours come from semantic roles, so the component flips with the theme and needs no dark-mode rules of its own. Re-theme it by overriding its component tokens on a parent, never by editing the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

Native radios in a <fieldset> with a <legend> (APG Radio Group).

Keyboard

KeyAction
↑ ↓ ← →Moves and selects.
SpaceSelects the focused radio.
TabEnters or leaves the group (one tab stop).

Focus

Ring on the checked radio (or the segmented card).

Labelling

Legend names the group; a single question per group.

Contrast

Selected label on surface
Control edge on surface
Focus ring on surface

Motion

Colour and background transitions use --duration-fast; under prefers-reduced-motion they are effectively instant.

Touch

Hit area is at least --target-min (24px) and 44px for primary mobile actions.

Content

Options are short, parallel nouns. Show the price delta beside the option.
