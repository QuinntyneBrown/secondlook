Form layout

How fields, summaries and action bars are arranged on a form.

Overview

Single column by default; fields stack with --space-4 between them and the primary action sits last, bottom-aligned on phones.

Anatomy

  1. Error summaryTop of form after a failed submit, links to each field.
  2. FieldsStacked .field wrappers.
  3. Actions bar.form-actions: back left, primary right.

Sizes

Forms are one column; width follows the container.

States

Pointer and focus states are forced here with data-state so they can be reviewed without a mouse; real pages get them from :hover, :focus-visible and :active. Attribute states (aria-*, disabled) are the real ones.

Responsive behaviour

Inputs fill their container (never fixed width) and stack one per row below the medium breakpoint. Heights stay at 40px or more so touch targets are comfortable.

Theming

Colours come from semantic roles, so the component flips with the theme and needs no dark-mode rules of its own. Re-theme it by overriding its component tokens on a parent, never by editing the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

A native form with novalidate and custom messages; summary is a focus target after submit.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to or from the control in reading order.
EnterSubmits from a text field.

Focus

After a failed submit, focus moves to the summary; its links jump to each field.

Labelling

Summary heading states the count; links use the field label and the fix.

Contrast

Summary text
Body text on surface

Motion

Colour and background transitions use --duration-fast; under prefers-reduced-motion they are effectively instant.

Touch

Hit area is at least --target-min (24px) and 44px for primary mobile actions.

Content

Summary: “Fix 2 things to continue”. Button: the next step (Continue, Pay $50.00).
