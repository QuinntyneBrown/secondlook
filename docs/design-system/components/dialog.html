Dialog

A modal window for a focused task or confirmation.

WCAG 2.2 AAStatus: stable

Overview

Use for confirmations and short forms (Add another car, Share report). Not for long content (use a page) or passive information (use an alert). Button-triggered editing always opens a dialog or navigates.

Anatomy

  1. BackdropDims and inerts the page.
  2. Icon (optional)Info or danger.
  3. Titlearia-labelledby.
  4. BodyDescription and fields.
  5. FooterCancel left of the primary action.

Variants

Information and destructive.

States

Dialogs share the button states. While busy, the primary button shows a spinner and the rest of the dialog is disabled.

Responsive behaviour

Below the medium breakpoint the panel sits at the bottom edge as a full-width sheet; from medium it is centred.

Theming

Colours come from semantic roles, so the component flips with the theme and needs no dark-mode rules of its own. Re-theme it by overriding its component tokens on a parent, never by editing the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

APG Dialog (Modal): role="dialog" aria-modal="true", labelled and described.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabCycles inside the dialog.
EscCloses (unless busy) and returns focus.
EnterActivates the focused button.

Focus

Focus moves to the first field or the least destructive button; the page is inert; focus returns to the trigger on close.

Labelling

Title names it; description via aria-describedby.

Contrast

Panel text
Danger icon
Focus ring on surface

Motion

Panel fades and rises over --duration-base; reduced motion shows it instantly.

Touch

Hit area is at least --target-min (24px) and 44px for primary mobile actions.

Content

Title is a question or verb phrase. Primary button repeats the verb: “Cancel inspection”, not “OK”.
