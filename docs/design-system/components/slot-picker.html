Slot picker

A grid of time slots for scheduling the inspection.

Overview

Radio-based grid; unavailable slots are struck through and disabled.

Anatomy

  1. SlotTime.
  2. SelectedAccent outline.
  3. UnavailableStruck through.

Variants

Slots.

Grid
HTML
<div class="slot-grid" role="radiogroup" aria-label="Choose a time" style="width:100%"><label class="slot"><input type="radio" name="sl" checked>6:00 PM</label><label class="slot"><input type="radio" name="sl">6:30 PM</label><label class="slot"><input type="radio" name="sl" disabled>7:00 PM</label></div>

Sizes

Slots are 44px tall.

States

Pointer and focus states are forced here with data-state so they can be reviewed without a mouse; real pages get them from :hover, :focus-visible and :active. Attribute states (aria-*, disabled) are the real ones.

VariantDefaultFocusDisabled
Slot

Both themes

Responsive behaviour

Domain components follow the page template that hosts them; they reflow to one column below the medium breakpoint.

Theming

Colours come from semantic roles, so the component flips with the theme and needs no dark-mode rules of its own. Re-theme it by overriding its component tokens on a parent, never by editing the component.

Motion

Colour and background transitions use --duration-fast; under prefers-reduced-motion they are effectively instant.

Touch

Hit area is at least --target-min (24px) and 44px for primary mobile actions.
