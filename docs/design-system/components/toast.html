Toast

A brief, non-blocking message about something that just happened.

Overview

Use for confirmation of an action. Errors that need a decision persist. Never the only place a critical message appears.

Anatomy

  1. IconTone.
  2. Title and textWhat happened.
  3. Action (optional)Undo, View, Retry.
  4. DismissLabelled button.

Sizes

States

Pointer and focus states are forced here with data-state so they can be reviewed without a mouse; real pages get them from :hover, :focus-visible and :active. Attribute states (aria-*, disabled) are the real ones.

Responsive behaviour

Feedback is full-width on phones and anchored to a corner from the medium breakpoint, never covering the focused control.

Theming

Colours come from semantic roles, so the component flips with the theme and needs no dark-mode rules of its own. Re-theme it by overriding its component tokens on a parent, never by editing the component.

Accessibility

Role and pattern

role="status" (polite) or role="alert" for errors, in a live region.

Keyboard

KeyAction
Tab / Shift+TabMoves focus to or from the control in reading order.
EscDismisses the focused toast.

Focus

Toasts never take focus; the action is reachable by Tab.

Labelling

Title first, then detail.

Contrast

Toast text
Info icon on inverse
Success icon on inverse
Warning icon on inverse
Danger icon on inverse

Motion

Enters with --ease-enter; timer of 8s pauses on hover and focus; errors persist.

Touch

Hit area is at least --target-min (24px) and 44px for primary mobile actions.

Content

Say what happened in one line; one action at most.
