# SecondLook elevator pitch

**Know the cost before you commit.**

Buying a used car as is? The asking price is only part of the story. SecondLook connects buyers with a local mechanic over live video to estimate what it could cost to get the car safety certified. The first inspection is fifty dollars; each additional car in the same fourteen-day window is twenty-five. Buyers can add a body shop review, compare options, and know the potential cost before they commit.

## The offer

- $50 for the first mechanic inspection.
- $25 for each additional vehicle in the same 14-day window.
- Optional body shop review: +$20 per inspection.
- A written estimate to help buyers compare vehicles. Prices before applicable tax.
