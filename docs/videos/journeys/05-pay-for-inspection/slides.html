Server-owned prices, idempotent payments and a local payment adapter

Real app

Quote, invalid, declined, paid

Local payment adapter: no real card, no real charge.

API

One checkout, one charge

Card details go to the processor and come back as a token.

Refund rules

  • No-show, incomplete inspection, cancel more than 2 h ahead: full refund
  • Cancel under 2 h ahead: $25 fee kept
  • Report delivered: not refundable
  • Covered by automated tests; booking journeys call them

Things to remember

  • The server decides every price
  • A payment goes through once, however often it is sent
  • Card numbers never touch our database
