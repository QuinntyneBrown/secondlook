# 01 · Booking an inspection

Buying a used car as is, and can't get a mechanic out to see it? This video shows how you book a SecondLook video inspection, from the first tap to your confirmation. It takes about two minutes. You need the car's details and a time that works for the seller.

## The car

Start with the car. Enter the VIN, the odometer reading, and the asking price. SecondLook reads the VIN and tells you what it found, here a twenty sixteen Honda Civic LX. Add the listing link if you have one, then the seller's name and phone number. We text the seller a link to join the call, so there is nothing for them to install.

## The review

Next, choose the review. A mechanic gives you the cost to get the car safety certified. A body shop looks at rust, paint, and panel damage. Or take both in a single call for twenty dollars more. Your order is shown beside the form as you go, so the total never surprises you.

## The time

Now pick a time. Choose a day, then a time when the seller can be with the car. Dev Patel is a licensed mechanic in Hamilton, with over four hundred inspections behind them. Once you choose a slot, we hold it for ten minutes.

## Paying

Then pay. Your first inspection is fifty dollars, and every additional car in the same window is twenty five. In this example the Civic is an additional car, plus twenty dollars for both reviews, so the total is forty five dollars. Confirm that you have told the seller about the video call, and pay. If the seller doesn't join, you are refunded in full.

## Booked

And you are booked. The confirmation shows the day, the time, and who is inspecting. We text the seller, with a reminder an hour before. Your estimate lands within an hour of the call. Comparing more than one car? You can add another from this page for twenty five dollars, until your window closes.

Here is what to remember. Booking takes four steps: the car, the review, the time, and payment. The first inspection is fifty dollars, and each additional car in the window is twenty five. If the seller doesn't join, you are refunded in full. Next, we join the live inspection.
