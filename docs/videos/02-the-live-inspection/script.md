# 02 · The live inspection

It's the day of your inspection. This video follows you from the inspection page, through the lobby, into the call, and out the other side with your report on its way.

## Before the call

Open the inspection from your list. It shows the booking, the car, the seller, and your team: the mechanic and, if you added one, a body shop reviewer. The call opens fifteen minutes before the start time. When it does, choose join the call.

## The lobby

The lobby is where you check your camera and microphone. You can also see who is here. The mechanic is ready, the seller joins from the text we sent, and the body shop reviewer joins a little later. The call is recorded so the mechanic can write your report, and everyone is told before it starts.

## On the call

On the call, the mechanic works through the car while the seller holds the camera. A checklist shows how far along they are. Here the brakes and tires are done, and suspension is in progress, with steering, lights, and body panels still to come. The mechanic's notes appear as they go. For example, the pads look thin on the front driver side, so the seller is asked to point the camera at the rotor. You can mute yourself at any time.

If your connection drops, you see a message that we are reconnecting you. The mechanic and the seller can still hear each other, and the message clears on its own when you are back, usually within ten seconds.

## Ending the call

When the mechanic is done, end the call. We ask you to confirm first. The summary shows how long the call ran, twenty one minutes here, and what was checked: brakes, tires, suspension, steering, lights, and body. It also says what could not be seen. In this case, the underbody, because the car was not on a lift. Your report arrives by email and notification within an hour, and tells you what it costs to get the car safety certified.

## Recap

Here is what to remember. The call opens fifteen minutes before the start time. Check your camera and microphone in the lobby. The call is recorded for your report, and everyone is told. If your connection drops, you are reconnected automatically. Next, we read the safety estimate.
