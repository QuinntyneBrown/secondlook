# L2 - Detailed Requirements

Copy in quotation marks is taken verbatim from `docs/mocks/`. Requirements marked **Source: journeys only** have no mock; their criteria restate `docs/user-journeys.md` and nothing more.

## L2-001: Home page
**Traces to:** L1-001

The home page introduces the service, shows the three steps, a sample report, and the pricing teaser.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a visitor who is not signed in, when they open the home page, then the page must show the heading "A mechanic’s second look, before you commit." and the line "First inspection $50. Each additional car in the same window, $25."
2. Given the home page, when the visitor selects "Book an inspection", then they must go to the book-vehicle page; when they select "View sample report", then they must go to the report page.
3. Given the home page, when it loads, then it must show the section "Three steps to a clear number" with the steps "Book a slot", "Walk the car on video" and "Get the number".
4. Given the home page, when it loads, then the sample report must show "Estimated cost to get it safety certified" with "$1,180–$1,540" and the findings "Front brake pads & rotors", "Rear tires", "Lower control arm bushing", "Windshield chip" and "Lights, horn, wipers" with the statuses Required, Required, Recommended, Recommended and Passes.
5. Given the home page, when it loads, then the header must show "How it works", "Pricing", "Help" and "Sign in", and the footer must show "Terms", "Privacy" and "Help".

## L2-002: Pricing page
**Traces to:** L1-001

The pricing page shows the price cards, a multi-vehicle calculator and pricing questions.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
1. Given a visitor, when they open the pricing page, then it must show the cards "First inspection" at "$50", "Each additional car" at "$25" and "Mechanic + body shop" at "+$20".
2. Given the calculator shows 3 vehicles, when the page loads, then it must show "3 vehicles", a total of "$100" and "You save $50".
3. Given the calculator, when the visitor selects "More vehicles" or "Fewer vehicles", then the vehicle count must change and the total must equal $50 plus $25 for each vehicle after the first.
4. Given the pricing page, when it loads, then the questions "How long is the window?", "When am I charged?" and "Is there a fee for the report?" must be shown as collapsed items, and selecting one must show its answer.

## L2-006: Create an account
