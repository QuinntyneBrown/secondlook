# L1 - High-Level Requirements

Sources: `docs/user-journeys.md`, the mocks under `docs/mocks/`, and the project overview in `AGENTS.md`.
L1-016 to L1-019 come from `docs/user-journeys.md` only; no mocks exist for them yet.

## L1-001: Public information pages
The system must give visitors, without signing in, pages that explain the service, its pricing, its policies and where to get help.

## L1-002: Account creation and authentication
The system must let a buyer, mechanic or body shop create an account, verify their email, sign in, recover a forgotten password and sign out.

## L1-003: Account settings
The system must let a signed-in buyer manage their profile, how they are notified, their payment methods and the deletion of their account.

## L1-004: Inspection dashboard and history
The system must give a buyer one place to see their current and past inspections, their status, and the state of their pricing window.

## L1-005: Book an inspection
The system must let a buyer book a video inspection by describing the vehicle and seller, choosing the type of review, and choosing a time with an available mechanic.

## L1-006: Pricing, payment and booking confirmation
The system must price each booking by one set of rules, take payment for it safely, confirm the booking, keep a receipt history, and refund according to the refund rules.

## L1-007: Additional vehicles within the pricing window
The system must let a buyer add further vehicles inside the pricing window at the reduced price, and keep the buyer informed about when the window closes.

## L1-008: Manage an upcoming inspection
The system must let a buyer view an inspection, reschedule it, cancel it, and see a cancelled inspection afterwards.

## L1-009: Live video inspection
The system must let the buyer check their camera and microphone, join the live video inspection with the mechanic and seller, follow the inspection checklist, recover from connection problems, and end the call.

## L1-010: Safety certification estimate report
The system must give the buyer a written report from the mechanic with the cost to get the vehicle safety certified, with an optional body shop review, and let the buyer share it, download it and challenge it.

## L1-011: Compare vehicle estimates
The system must let a buyer compare the estimates for the vehicles in the same window side by side.

## L1-012: Notifications
The system must tell the buyer about events that matter to them, through a notification list, transient toasts, banners and a cookie consent prompt.

## L1-013: Mechanic profile
The system must let a buyer see who their mechanic is, including their credentials and recent buyer feedback.

## L1-014: Access control
The system must show each user only the data they are entitled to see and must tell users clearly when they are not.

## L1-015: System states
The system must show clear pages when a page is missing, the system fails, the user is offline or the system is down for maintenance.

## L1-016: Mechanic and body shop availability
The system must let a mechanic or body shop set the times they can take inspections, so buyers can book only those times.

## L1-017: Body shop review
The system must let a body shop review a vehicle over video and submit its findings to the buyer.

## L1-018: Saved vehicles
The system must let a buyer add, edit and remove their saved vehicles.

## L1-019: Administer mechanics and body shops
The system must let an administrator onboard, approve and deactivate mechanics and body shops.

## L1-020: Dialogs
The system must present confirmations and short edits as modal dialogs that behave the same way for loading, failure and keyboard use.
