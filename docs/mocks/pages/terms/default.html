Terms of service

Last updated 1 October 2026.

What we provide

SecondLook arranges a video inspection of a used vehicle by an independent, licensed mechanic and gives you a written estimate. An estimate is an opinion on the day of the call, not a guarantee of a future safety standard result.

What you agree to

You agree to book only vehicles you have the seller’s permission to inspect, and to share the report only with people you choose.

Payments and refunds

You pay at booking. If the seller doesn’t join, or the mechanic can’t complete the inspection, you’re refunded in full. Once a report is delivered it is not refundable.

Limits

We can’t see what the camera can’t show. The mechanic will say what they couldn’t check, and the report lists it.
