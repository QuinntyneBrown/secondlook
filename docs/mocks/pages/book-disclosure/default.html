What you’re getting

The mechanic gives you an estimate of what it would cost to get this car safety certified. It is the mechanic’s opinion on the day, based on the information, photos and video you provide. It is not a guarantee that the car will pass, or that the final repair bill will match.

What can change the number

  • Hidden damage and parts the camera can’t show, like brake lines or a cracked frame.
  • Work found only once the car is on a hoist.
  • Shop labour and parts prices that differ from the estimate.

What the mechanic needs

Have these ready before the call. The seller can send them, or show them on camera.

  • Vehicle details. Year, make, model and VIN. We have these from the Vehicle step.
  • Photos. All four sides, the interior, the odometer, each tyre, the engine bay and the VIN plate.
  • Video with the engine running. A cold start, idle and the dash lights.

The car must be running during the call.
