How SecondLook works

From booking to a clear number, usually inside a day.

  1. Book

    Enter the VIN, odometer and the seller’s phone. Choose a mechanic review, a body shop review, or both.

  2. Schedule

    Pick a time that works for you and the seller. We confirm a local mechanic and send the seller a link to join.

  3. Video call

    The seller walks the car while the mechanic checks it live. You can watch, ask questions and listen in.

  4. Report

    Within an hour of the call you get a findings list and a cost range to get the car safety certified.

Questions buyers ask

Does the seller need an app?

No. They open a link on their phone, allow the camera, and walk the car. Nothing to install.

What does “safety certified” cover?

In Ontario, the safety standard inspection a car must pass before it can be plated. The estimate covers what would fail it and what is worth fixing.

Can I add a body shop review?

Yes. Add it to any inspection. Choosing both is a small extra fee, and one call covers mechanical and body.

What if the seller cancels?

If the call doesn’t happen, you aren’t charged. Reschedule or cancel from the inspection page.
