How can we help?

Answers from the people who run the inspections.

What if the seller doesn’t show up?

You’re refunded in full. We’ll offer to reschedule, but you don’t have to.

How do refunds work?

Cancel up to 2 hours before the call for a full refund. Later than that, we refund $25 and keep $25 for the mechanic’s time.

Can I share the report with the seller?

Yes. Use Share on the report page. The link works for 14 days unless you turn it off.

Does the report guarantee the car will pass?

No. It’s a mechanic’s opinion on the day, based on what the camera could show. The report lists anything the mechanic couldn’t check.

What counts as the same window?

Fourteen days from your first booking. Cars added inside it are $25 each.

Still stuck?

Ask us. A person answers within a few hours, 8 AM to 8 PM.
