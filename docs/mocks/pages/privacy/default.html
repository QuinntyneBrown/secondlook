Privacy policy

Last updated 1 October 2026.

What we collect

Your name, email, payment details (held by our payment processor), the vehicle details you enter, and the call recording needed to produce your report.

Call recordings

Calls are recorded so the mechanic can write the report. Recordings are kept for 30 days, then deleted. The seller is told before the call begins.

Who sees your report

You do. If you share it, only the people you send the link to can open it, and you can turn the link off at any time.

Your choices

You can export or delete your data from Settings. Deleting your account removes your reports within 30 days.
