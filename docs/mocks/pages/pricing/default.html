Pricing questions

How long is the window?

Fourteen days from your first booking. Cars added inside the window cost $25 each.

When am I charged?

At booking. If the call can’t happen because the seller doesn’t show, you’re refunded in full.

Is there a fee for the report?

No. The report is included and stays in your account.
